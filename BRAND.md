# AMATERAS — Brand Guide

## Brand name

**AMATERAS**

## Brand idea

A modern Japanese weather and disaster map inspired by the idea of observing the sky and illuminating risk.

Do not turn the branding into a literal shrine/mythology theme.

The mythology reference should remain subtle.

## Critical semantic-color rule

The palette does not replace scientific/severity scales.

Examples:

- precipitation intensity keeps a meteorologically understandable spectrum
- official warning levels keep meaningful distinction
- hazard risk maps keep source-appropriate semantic colors

Brand colors belong primarily to the interface shell.

---

## Logo direction

Source:

`references/palette_C_logo_reference.png`

Keep:

- all-caps `AMATERAS`
- highly geometric wordmark
- clean white/silver lettering
- small golden solar arc above the central portion

Avoid:

- large sun icon
- shrine imagery
- anime mythology imagery
- highly decorative Japanese motifs
- gradient-heavy logo effects

---

## Brand personality

- precise
- calm
- trustworthy
- observant
- modern
- Japanese without cliché
- technical without military cosplay

---

## Copy direction

Preferred language:

- short
- factual
- calm
- source-aware

Avoid dramatic marketing copy inside the radar application itself.

Examples:

Good:
- `LIVE`
- `最終更新 10:32`
- `現在、重大な警報はありません`
- `気象庁発表`

Bad:
- `AIが未来を予測しています`
- `日本を守る究極の気象知能`
- `危険を完全予測`
